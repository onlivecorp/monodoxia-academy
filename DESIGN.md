## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the contemplative luxury, scientific rigor, and transformative human depth of an elite personal development and psychological institution. Operating at the confluence of academic authority and spiritual serenity, the aesthetic discards transactional tech trends and aggressive conversion patterns in favor of calm, prestige, and spacious editorial discipline. 

The target audience comprises discerning individuals, executives, scholars, and seekers who demand absolute intellectual credibility paired with an atmosphere of psychological safety and profound self-actualization. The UI evokes sanctuary, clarity, and enduring prestige—akin to a private research salon or an architectural wellness retreat.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Sanctuary
The style merges **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Classical Proportions**:
- **Generous Spatial Rhythms**: Whitespace acts not merely as padding, but as emotional silence and cognitive breathing room.
- **Architectural Line Art**: Fine 1px champagne hair lines, delicate dividers, and classical proportions govern layouts instead of heavy fills or dropshadows.
- **Quiet Luxury**: Color is deployed with radical restraint. Midnight obsidian grounds the intellect, alabaster provides warmth, and warm champagne gold is reserved strictly for focal accents, achievements, and structural highlights.

## Colors

The palette is tuned to establish authority, cognitive ease, and discreet opulence. Light mode is the default state, presenting an inviting, warm alabaster canvas that feels tactile and literary.

### Primary: Midnight Obsidian & Deep Navy
- **Primary Deep (`#070F1E`)**: Grounding contrast, modal scrims, and profound header treatments.
- **Primary Core (`#0A192F`)**: Master text color, primary interactive surfaces, dark card backgrounds, and core branding.
- **Primary Surface (`#112240`)**: Elevated dark containers, hovered dark button states, and deep navigational banners.

### Secondary: Noble Champagne Gold & Warm Ochre
- **Gold Deep (`#9E782F`)**: Pressed states, high-contrast text on alabaster, and intricate linework.
- **Gold Core (`#C5A059`)**: Primary accent, active status borders, pill badges, and refined iconography.
- **Gold Warm (`#D4AF37`)**: Focal highlights, awards, and golden ratio geometric accents.
- **Gold Light (`#DFBE77`)**: Soft hover states, subtle badge backdrops, and active tab indicator rules.

### Backgrounds & Neutrals: Warm Editorial Alabaster
- **Canvas (`#FBF9F5`)**: Primary application canvas; an organic, anti-glare warm white.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#F5F1E9`)**: Soft section contrast, foundational neutral cards, and input backgrounds.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#EFEAE1`)**: Secondary panels, subtle structural insets, and hover fills.
- **Elevated White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Card surfaces needing focal lift and crisp readability.

### Borders & Structural Linework
- **Border Champagne Muted (`#E6DFC6`)**: Primary hairline borders for cards, inputs, and architectural rules.
- **Border Subtle Cream (`#E2D9C8`)**: Light structural dividers, secondary list separators, and subtle table rows.
- **Navy Ghost Border (`rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.08)`)**: Low-contrast contrast rings on elevated white surfaces.

### Text Hierarchy
- **Text Primary (`#0A192F`)**: Headlines, body copy, and high-emphasis elements.
- **Text Secondary (`#4A5B73`)**: Subtitles, body secondary, timestamps, and metadata.
- **Text Tertiary (`#8A99AD`)**: Disabled states, footnotes, and quiet watermarks.
- **Text Inverted (`#FBF9F5`)**: Text displayed over Midnight Obsidian containers.

## Typography

The typographical voice strikes a dual equilibrium: **Playfair Display** introduces academic heritage, psychological gravitas, and literary presence, while **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides razor-sharp clarity, accessibility, and modern usability.

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Leading**: Body line heights are deliberately open (1.6 to 1.7 ratio) to cultivate effortless, unhurried reading during intensive self-reflection or clinical curriculum study.
- **Letter Spacing Discipline**: Display headings feature slight negative tracking for sculpted cohesion, whereas uppercase labels and badges utilize deliberate positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) for understated European luxury.
- **Editorial Italicization**: Playfair Display’s italics are reserved for key psychological affirmations, academic subtitles, or reflective quotes, infusing human warmth into clinical precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is rooted in architectural symmetry, generous white space, and the golden ratio. Interface sections are given room to breathe, preventing intellectual fatigue.

### Spatial Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width: 1360px)**: Anchored by a 12-column grid with a `1.5rem` (24px) gutter and `3rem` (48px) margins. Standard content layouts occupy 8 or 10 centered columns to ensure optimal typographic measure.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1024px)**: Fluid 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px)**: 4-column structure with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Micro-Gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`)**: Used for cohesive atomic relationships—label-to-input pairings, icon-text clusters, and badge padding.
- **Structural Flow (`space-lg`, `space-xl`)**: Applied to internal card padding, module separations, and list items.
- **Sanctuary Breathing Room (`space-2xl`, `space-3xl`)**: Dedicated strictly to outer section transitions, thematic course modules, and meditative quote blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid generic SaaS gloss and heavy drop shadows, this design system establishes depth through **tactile tonal layering, architectural hairline borders, and whisper-soft ambient warmth**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Canvas (Base Ground)**: `#FBF9F5`. Flat, calming, paper-like foundation.
2. **Structural Recessed**: `#F5F1E9`. Subdued module regions, input fields, and quiet filter bars.
3. **Primary Surface (Elevated Card)**: `#FFFFFF`. Paired with a delicate 1px border (`#E6DFC6` or `rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06)`). Elevation is signaled via a diffuse ambient aura: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.03)`.
4. **Interactive Hover / Floating Layer**: Elevated modules gain subtle vertical lift and warm illumination: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.07), 0 2px 8px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.06)`.
5. **Architectural Obsidian (Modals & Command Layers)**: Deep `#0A192F` surfaces surrounded by a 1px border in `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)` and an expansive obsidian shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(7, 15, 30, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (Level 1)** shape language. Straight lines communicate institutional structure and academic discipline, while soft 0.25rem (4px) to 0.5rem (8px) corners temper the composition with humanist warmth.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Structured Cards**: `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius, preserving an architectural silhouette.
- **Large Panels & Course Portals**: `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- **Pill Badges & Circular Indicators**: `9999px` (Pill). Exclusively allocated to philosophical status indicators, micro-tags, module stage badges, and focal avatar markers to contrast against the grid’s rectilinear structure.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Midnight Obsidian)**: Background `#0A192F`, text `#FBF9F5`, border 1px solid `transparent`. On hover: `#112240` with an ambient shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.18)`.
- **Secondary (Noble Champagne)**: Background `transparent`, text `#0A192F`, border 1px solid `#C5A059`. On hover: background `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`, border color `#9E782F`.
- **Tertiary (Subtle Text/Editorial)**: Background `transparent`, text `#4A5B73`, uppercase tracking (`0.06em`). On hover: text `#0A192F` accompanied by an animated 1px gold bottom rule.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Compact geometry with full `rounded-full` curvature.
- Background `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`, text `#9E782F` in `label-sm` font token.
- Academy distinction badges feature a tiny 4px gold center dot preceding the label.

### Cards & Curriculum Modules
- Rendered on crisp `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F1E9` surfaces with a 1px border (`#E6DFC6`).
- Top hairline feature: Top edge may feature an optional 2px champagne gold rule (`#C5A059`) for featured masterclasses and psychological assessments.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2D9C8`, radius 4px, text `#0A192F`, placeholder `#8A99AD`.
- **Focus State**: Border color shifted to `#C5A059` accompanied by a soft halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`. No harsh primary blue rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square or circle housing with a 1.5px border (`#C5A059`). Selected states fill with `#0A192F` and display a crisp champagne gold interior mark.

### Lists & Curriculum Trees
- Separated by delicate 1px rules (`#E2D9C8`).
- Index numbering (e.g., `01`, `02`, `03`) styled in Playfair Display italic `#C5A059`, followed by course title in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Micro-Tabs & Segmented Selectors
- Minimalist underline tabs: Idle state text `#4A5B73`. Active tab marked by text `#0A192F` and an anchored 2px `#C5A059` indicator bar.
- Enclosed luxury switch: Background `#EFEAE1`, pill active card `#FFFFFF` with whisper shadow and 1px `#E6DFC6` outline.
